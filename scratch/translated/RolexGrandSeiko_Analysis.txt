This work aims to carry out an analysis that seeks to compare the presence
online (in the field of online advertising with an exclusive focus on Google Search Ads) of two
direct competitors of the chosen brand (OMEGA) for the final project which, in this case, are: Rolex and
Grand Seiko.
These brands have markedly different positions when it comes to the watchmaking ethos that
design, produce and sell, this Ethos, which also manifests itself in the way
build your Ads, from Headlines to Descriptions.

=== PAGE 28 ===
The Google Search Ads dispute between Rolex and Grand Seiko is not a race, but a confrontation
of philosophies. 
Rolex pays the price of Authority and Control (MOFU) by defending every pixel on the screen to
ensure that consideration is never tainted by action. 
On the other hand, Grand Seiko plays the Efficiency and ROI (BOFU) game, sacrificing the premium position
in order to, in return, achieve the objectives of the corporation. 
The digital thus becomes a battlefield where the Swiss “Unquestionable Status” meets the
“Ambitious Optimization” from Japan.
